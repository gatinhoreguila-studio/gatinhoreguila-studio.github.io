EJECT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

EJECT is made by Gatinho Reguila Unipessoal Lda (Porto, Portugal).

Your photos stay on your iPhone

EJECT turns photos you choose into a film-style photo book. All processing happens on your device. Your photos never leave it. We do not upload, store, or see them.

Data we collect

None. EJECT has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It does not use the network to process your photos.

Photo library

EJECT reads only the photos you pick, and asks permission to save finished pages back to your library. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Photo metadata

EJECT may read the date and location stored inside the photos you pick, and may recognise simple scene content such as animals, to choose page styles. This happens entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere or kept after the book is made.

Camera

If you use the in-app camera, pictures are kept only on your device.

Sharing

When you share a page, EJECT uses the standard iOS share sheet and may place a short hashtag on your clipboard. You decide where it goes.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Restore Purchases works through your Apple ID with Apple.

Children

EJECT is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
